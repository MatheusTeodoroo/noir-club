# Credits

**Corte da Minoria / Minority Court** is inspired by the "King Game" Main Match from season 2,
episode 3 of *The Genius: Rule Breaker* (더 지니어스: 룰 브레이커, tvN, South Korea, 2014).

- Only the game mechanics are adapted (a secret colour, a king splitting everyone into two
  groups, the minority of each group scoring, a single-use token to switch groups). Names, texts,
  art and the Noir Club presentation are original.
- Unofficial, non-commercial version; not affiliated with or endorsed by the show's producers.

## Changes from the TV game

- 5 to 15 players; as many rounds as players, everyone reigns exactly once.
- The first king is elected by secret vote (self-votes allowed); a tie goes to a runoff among the
  tied, and a second tie to a draw. Each king then names an heir who hasn't reigned.
- Colours are Blue and Green, chosen on the phone or at the Noir Club Dealer (Host option), with a
  keep-or-switch window before rounds 3, 5, 7…
- The single-use token is the **Royal Pass**, used publicly in a short window after the split; it
  may leave a hall with fewer than 2 players.
- A tie, a single colour or a lone player in a hall scores nobody there. The TV shows the colour
  counts per hall each round; points stay secret until the final reveal.
