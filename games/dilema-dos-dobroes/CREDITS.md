# Credits

**O Dilema dos Dobrões / The Doubloon Dilemma** is inspired by a Main Match from episode 8 of
*The Genius: Rules of the Game* (더 지니어스: 게임의 법칙, tvN, South Korea, 2013).

- Only the game mechanics are adapted (two teams, a burning pot, a team win against an individual
  ranking). Names, texts, art and the Noir Club presentation are original.
- Unofficial, non-commercial version; not affiliated with or endorsed by the show's producers.

## Changes from the TV game

- Any even number of players from 4 to 12, in two equal teams drawn at random.
- Deposits are made one player at a time at the Noir Club Dealer, with a visit time limit
  (30 s by default) and a deposit of 0 for anyone who runs out of time, leaves without
  confirming or never visits.
- Ties refund every deposit and replay the round; three ties in a row, or both teams running out
  of doubloons, end the match early.
- Room points: players − place + 1.
