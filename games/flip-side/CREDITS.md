# Credits

**Frente e Verso / Flip Side** is a free adaptation of the mechanics of a poker game with
double-sided cards played on the Korean TV show *The Genius* (tvN). Game rules and mechanics are
not protected by copyright; the show's name for the game, its presentation and its art are not
used here.

Changes for Noir Club:

- Two players; a 200-card shoe (four sets of 50 cards: the 45 distinct number pairs once plus 5
  random pairs, with a random front), reshuffled when it runs out; no used-card history.
- The side is picked with each player's first move and is public; the first player may check.
- Only one player per round may bet on both sides; a both-sides bet loses on any equal side.
- A turn timeout folds. Configurable starting chips, round limit with sudden death, and turn timer.

All names, text, layout and art are original to Noir Club. This is not an official version of any TV show or product.
