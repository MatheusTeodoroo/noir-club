# Credits

**Mercado das Sombras / Shadow Market** is an adaptation of the "Mystery Stock Market" game
played in **The Devil's Plan** (O Jogo do Diabo), season 3, episode 4 (Netflix).

- Kept from the original: 7 stocks opening at 10,000 (won → $, same values), minimum price 100,
  100,000 starting money, 24 rounds, hidden per-company price rules revealed in parts at rounds
  5, 10, 15 and fully at 20, the once-per-game Secret that hides a stock for two rounds, and the
  1.5M / 3M / 6M point bands.
- Changed: the game name, every company name and icon, and the rules themselves — each match
  draws 7 of 20 original rule templates with random numbers, checked by a balance simulation;
  no elimination; +1 point for 1st place; configurable round length and optional price noise.

This is a fan-made adaptation for a free, non-commercial party platform. It is not affiliated
with or endorsed by Netflix or the show's producers.
