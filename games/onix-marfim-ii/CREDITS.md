# Credits

**Ônix & Marfim II / Onyx & Ivory II** is an original Noir Club implementation of a classic
two-player hidden-bid duel: each player spends a shared-size budget of points over several rounds,
the higher bid wins each round, and only the bid's colour (one digit or two) and a coarse lamp of
the points left are ever shown. Game mechanics are not subject to copyright; no names, text or art
from any existing product are used.

Noir Club choices:

- One-digit bids are onyx, two-digit bids ivory; each player's points left show as a five-light lamp.
- The winner of a round starts the next; a tied round keeps the same starter.
- Five wins end the duel at once; equal wins after nine rounds start a tiebreak duel with 99 points again.
- The bids are revealed on the TV at the end of each duel.
- A turn timer bids 0 when it runs out; a player with no points left bids 0 automatically.

All text, layout and art are original to Noir Club. This is not an official version of any TV show or product.
