# Credits

**Ônix & Marfim / Onyx & Ivory** is an original Noir Club implementation of a classic two-player
hidden-number duel: each player holds the numbers 0–8, plays one per round, and only the colour
(even or odd) of the rival's choice is ever shown. Game mechanics are not subject to copyright;
no names, text or art from any existing product are used.

Noir Club choices:

- Even stones are onyx, odd stones are ivory; the TV shows each player's remaining stones by colour only.
- The winner of a round starts the next; a tied round keeps the same starter.
- The numbers are revealed on the TV at the end of each duel.
- Equal points after nine rounds start a tiebreak duel with fresh stones.
- A turn timer plays the lowest stone left when it runs out.

All text, layout and art are original to Noir Club. This is not an official version of any TV show or product.
