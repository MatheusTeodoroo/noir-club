# Credits

**Roleta Russa** is a fan adaptation of the game **"Russian Roulette"** played in the reality show
**The Devil's Plan** (*O Jogo do Diabo*), season 3, episode 5. It is not an official version and is
not affiliated with the show or its producers. No art, names or footage from the show are used: the
board, texts and visuals are original to Noir Club.

## Changes from the original
- 3 to 10 players instead of 6, with one chamber per player; the Host picks the number of bullets
  (default 2, at most players − 1).
- The firing starts when half the players (rounded up) are out of credits.
- Both spins are public; investigations reveal which chamber of the pair holds a bullet.
- A player who leaves the match counts as out of credits and passes automatically.
- Survivors score 1 point on the room scoreboard.
