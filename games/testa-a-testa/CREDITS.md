# Credits

**Testa a Testa / Browbeat** adapts the traditional card game known as *Blind Man's Bluff* (also
called "Indian Poker"), in which each player holds a card to their forehead so only the others can
see it. It is a folk game with no known author; its rules are in the public domain.

Changes for Noir Club:

- Two players, a deck of two 1–10 suits (20 cards) dealt without replacement, the used cards shown on the TV.
- Ante of 1 chip; Pagar (call) ends the betting, Aumentar (raise) continues it, Desistir (fold) concedes.
- Folding with a 10 on your brow pays a 10-chip penalty; equal cards carry the pot to the next round.
- Configurable starting chips, round limit with sudden death, and a turn timer.

All text, layout and art are original to Noir Club. This is not an official version of any TV show or product.
